# Aerospacelab

> Aerospacelab is a vertically integrated satellite manufacturer based in Mont-Saint-Guibert, Belgium, founded in 2018. It designs, builds, tests and operates small-to-medium satellites — the Versatile Satellite Platform (VSP-50, VSP-150, VSP-300) family plus mission-specific Earth observation, SAR, RF-sensing and telecommunications payloads — for commercial, institutional and government customers on an ITAR-free European supply chain.

Aerospacelab publishes **no public API, no developer portal and no machine-readable API contract**. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, MCP or A2A surface to call. Its programmatic capabilities (mission control software, a tasking tool, an image-processing pipeline and a data platform, built in part under the ESA InCubed MultiSpectral Companion Mission) are delivered to contracted mission customers, not to self-serve developers. Do not attempt to construct requests against an Aerospacelab API — none is documented.
